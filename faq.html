For Employers & Hiring Managers

Our search services operate strictly on a performance-backed contingency basis. You only incur a fee when a candidate we present is successfully hired and starts work at your organization. There are zero upfront fees, retains, or hidden administrative costs to initiate a search with our desk.

Our specialized focus on Controls, Civil, and Technical Legal practices allows us to maintain pre-vetted, active candidate networks. For standard technical requisitions, you can expect an initial vetted shortlist within 10 to 14 business days.

Every placement is backed by our standard 90-day replacement guarantee. If a placed candidate leaves or does not meet performance requirements within the guarantee window, we will source a qualified replacement candidate at no additional charge.

For Candidates & Job Seekers

No. Our career advisory, resume feedback, interview preparation, and placement services are 100% free for job seekers. All fees are paid entirely by our hiring employers.
